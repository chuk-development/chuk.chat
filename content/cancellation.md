## Cancelling Your Subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time:

1. **In the app:** Go to Settings → Subscription → Cancel
2. **By email:** Send a cancellation request to [support@chuk.chat](mailto:support@chuk.chat)

Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period. After cancellation, you keep any remaining AI credits on your account and can continue using the service until your credits run out.

## Refund Policy

We do not provide refunds for subscription payments. However:

- Any unused AI credits remain on your account after cancellation
- You can continue using the service with your remaining credits
- Credits do not expire

If you have questions about your subscription or need assistance, please contact us at [support@chuk.chat](mailto:support@chuk.chat).
