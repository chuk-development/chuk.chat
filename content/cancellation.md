## Cancelling Your Subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time:

1. **In the app:** Go to Settings → Subscription → Cancel
2. **By email:** Send a cancellation request to [support@chuk.chat](mailto:support@chuk.chat)

Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period. After cancellation, you can keep using your remaining AI credits until the end of that billing period. Credits that are still unused at the end of the period expire.

## Refund Policy

We do not provide refunds for subscription payments. However:

- Your remaining AI credits stay usable until the end of the current billing period
- You can continue using the service with them until then
- Credits that are still unused at the end of the billing period expire

If you have questions about your subscription or need assistance, please contact us at [support@chuk.chat](mailto:support@chuk.chat).
